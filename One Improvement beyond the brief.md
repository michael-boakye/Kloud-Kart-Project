# RECOMMENDATION: INTRODUCE A CENTRALISED DATA GOVERNANCE PROCESS

I recommend that Kloud Kart introduce a centralised process for managing and controlling company data across its departments.

Currently, information is handled separately by Operations, Finance and Marketing. This creates risks such as inconsistent management figures, uncontrolled customer data exports and uncertainty about who is responsible for the accuracy and protection of business information.

The proposed process would establish clear ownership of data, approved access arrangements and common rules for creating, sharing, storing and reporting information.

## How Kloud Kart Should Incorporate It

- **Assign data ownership:** Each department should be responsible for the accuracy and proper use of the information it manages.
- **Standardise reporting:** Operations and Finance should use agreed definitions and reporting procedures when preparing management figures.
- **Control customer information:** Marketing should record customer data exports, including their purpose, responsible person, storage location and recipients.
- **Introduce regular reviews:** Department heads should periodically review data access, reporting accuracy and compliance with the agreed procedures.

## Why This Is Relevant to Kloud Kart

This recommendation addresses more than the immediate reporting problems. It establishes accountability for company information as Kloud Kart grows and expands into other markets.

It will help management work with consistent figures, make it easier to trace customer information, reduce unnecessary duplication and demonstrate that personal data is being handled responsibly under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Most importantly, it can be introduced using the company's existing systems and departmental responsibilities. It does not require an expensive data platform or an additional hire.
