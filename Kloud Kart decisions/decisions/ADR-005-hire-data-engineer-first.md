# ADR-005: Hire the Data Engineer Before the Reporting Analyst

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Decision owner:** Nana Adjei, Head of Operations

## Context

Kloud Kart has funding for two data hires this financial year. It currently has no dedicated data team, and its reporting arrangements are unreliable. The company needs both a reliable reporting foundation and better management reports.

## Decision

Kloud Kart will recruit a Data Engineer first, followed by a Business Intelligence and Reporting Analyst. The first hire will establish and protect the reporting environment, while the second will develop consistent management reports.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative A: Hire the Reporting Analyst first

- **How it would work:** The company would recruit someone to develop management reports and dashboards before appointing a specialist to establish the reporting environment.
- **Why it is attractive:** Nana and Finance could begin addressing their reporting differences sooner.
- **Why rejected:** The analyst would be working with an environment that has not yet been separated from the live website workload or properly organised for reliable reporting. This risks improving the presentation of figures without resolving the underlying problems.

## Consequences

- **What this buys:** The company establishes the technical foundation before expanding reporting and analysis. It also gives each hire a clear responsibility.
- **What this costs:** The company may have to wait longer for improved dashboards while the first hire establishes the reporting environment.
- **What it costs the client if I am wrong:** Delaying the analyst could postpone improvements to management reporting, especially if the first hire takes longer than expected to establish the reporting environment.
- **What would make me revisit this:** Difficulty recruiting a suitably qualified Data Engineer, a change in business priorities or evidence that the existing technical environment is already adequate for the analyst to begin safely.

## What this survives

The two-hire limit remains respected. If growth or investor pressure increases, Kloud Kart will have a dedicated person responsible for the reporting foundation before expanding management reporting. The company can increase reporting capability without immediately creating a need for a third data hire.
